Политика конфиденциальности — Jellyfish Cat Math

Дата вступления в силу: 2026-10-11

1. О политике

Эта политика объясняет обработку информации в Android-игре Jellyfish Cat Math («Счёт кота-медузы»), разработчик Vibe Captain, Package ID ru.vibecaptain.jellyfishcatmath, и на странице её политики конфиденциальности. Сайт разработчика: https://vibecaptain.ru/.

2. Информация на устройстве

В локальном хранилище WebView сохраняются результаты упражнений, очки, количество верных и неверных ответов, длительность и даты сессий, история игр, достижения, открытые навыки, коллекция персонажей, награды и настройки. Также локально сохраняются язык, прохождение обучения и предпочтения уведомлений об обновлениях. Это позволяет помнить прогресс и настройки; игровой код не отправляет эти записи Vibe Captain или Firebase.

В закрытом хранилище приложения также сохраняются полученная конфигурация, время проверки обновлений и учётные данные установки SDK. Резервное копирование и перенос Android могут копировать подходящие данные в зависимости от устройства, ОС и ваших настроек. В приложении разрешено резервное копирование Android; точный состав определяет система. Поэтому локальное хранение не означает гарантии, что системная резервная копия никогда не покидает устройство.

3. Передача для конфигурации и обновлений

Для получения параметров обновлений, включая доступную и минимальную версии, используются Google Firebase Remote Config и зависимость Firebase Installations. Они создают идентификатор конкретной установки — Firebase installation ID (FID) — и данные аутентификации. FID обозначает установку приложения, а не имя или аппаратный идентификатор, но остаётся идентификатором, обрабатываемым Google.

Запросы Remote Config содержат FID и токен аутентификации установки; идентификаторы приложения, имя пакета, версию и номер сборки; отпечаток сертификата подписи приложения; версии SDK и платформы Android; язык, код страны/региона и часовой пояс из настроек устройства. Firebase Installations также передаёт технические сведения о приложении, SDK и устройстве. Сетевое соединение раскрывает сервису IP-адрес подключения; Google может обрабатывать его как служебную информацию. Код региона в проверенной версии Remote Config берётся из локали устройства, а не GPS. Игра не запрашивает разрешение на геолокацию и намеренно не определяет ваше местоположение.

Google Play in-app updates обрабатывает технические сведения об устройстве, версию приложения и список установленных модулей и пакетов ресурсов для определения доступности и размера обновления. Установка выполняется сервисом Google Play. Игра не читает учётные данные вашего аккаунта Google.

Проверки конфигурации и обновлений запускаются автоматически при использовании приложения; отдельного выключателя сетевых запросов SDK в игре нет. При недоступности сервисов может использоваться кэш. Ответы на упражнения и сохранённый прогресс в эти запросы не включаются.

Privacy Policy — Jellyfish Cat Math

Effective date: 2026-10-11

1. About this policy

This policy explains how Jellyfish Cat Math (also known as «Счёт кота-медузы»), developed by Vibe Captain, handles information. It covers the Android game with package name ru.vibecaptain.jellyfishcatmath and this game's privacy page. Developer website: https://vibecaptain.ru/.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves exercise results, scores, correct and incorrect answer totals, session durations and dates, play history, achievements, unlocked skills, collected characters, rewards and game settings in the app's local WebView storage. Language selection, tutorial status and update-prompt preferences are also stored locally. These records let the game remember progress and preferences; the game does not upload these progress records to Vibe Captain or Firebase.

The app also stores downloaded configuration values, update-check timestamps and SDK installation credentials in its app-private storage. Android backup and device-transfer services may copy eligible app data depending on the device, operating system and your backup settings. The app allows Android backup; the exact data included depends on that system. Local storage therefore should not be interpreted as a guarantee that operating-system backups never leave the device.

3. Information transmitted for configuration and updates

The game uses Google Firebase Remote Config and its Firebase Installations dependency to obtain update settings, such as available and minimum app versions. These services generate an installation-specific identifier (Firebase installation ID, or FID) and authentication credentials. A FID identifies an installation, rather than being a hardware identifier or a name, but it is still an identifier processed by Google.

Remote Config requests include the FID and installation authentication token; app identifiers, package name, version and build number; the app signing-certificate fingerprint; SDK and Android platform versions; and the device's configured language, region/country code and time zone. Firebase Installations also sends technical app, SDK and device metadata. Network connections expose the connecting IP address to the service; Google may process it as operational service data. The region code sent by the audited Remote Config SDK comes from the device locale, not GPS. The game does not request location permission or intentionally determine your geographic location.

Google Play in-app updates processes device metadata, application version and the list of installed modules and asset packs to determine update availability and download size. Google Play handles update installation through its own service. The game does not read your Google account credentials.

Configuration and update checks run automatically while the app is in use; there is no in-game switch to opt out of SDK network requests. Cached configuration can be used when the services are unavailable. Exercise answers and saved progress are not included in these requests.

4. Services and uses

Google provides Firebase Remote Config, Firebase Installations and Google Play update services. Their processing supports configuration delivery, installation authentication and software updates; Google also processes technical service information to operate, secure and improve its services under its applicable terms. Links to Google's published information appear below.

The audited game contains no advertising SDK, Google Analytics for Firebase, Crashlytics, account registration, cloud leaderboard, chat or in-app payment integration. It does not request your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data. It does not use an advertising ID, Android ID, IMEI or serial number in the audited game code. An SDK installation ID and local gameplay record IDs should not be confused with these hardware or advertising identifiers.

Opening the privacy link uses a browser. The request to vibecaptain.ru exposes ordinary web-request information, including IP address, browser information, requested page and access time, to the website host. This privacy page contains no analytics script, advertising, third-party font download or language-preference cookie. The website is hosted on a VPS using nginx. The hosting provider and the actual server-log retention period have not yet been confirmed by the developer. If you contact the developer, the contact method you choose necessarily transmits your message and return-contact details to that channel.

5. Retention and security

Game progress remains in app storage until you reset it, clear app data, uninstall the game, or it is otherwise removed by the operating system. Play history is limited by the current game code to the most recent 3,000 session records; other progress has no fixed time-based expiry. Backup copies may remain under the rules and settings of the backup provider.

Firebase and Google Play retain service data under their own documented retention and deletion processes. Removing local game data does not by itself submit a Firebase server-side deletion request. Google publishes a separate Firebase installation-deletion procedure and retention information; this game currently has no button that invokes that procedure. This policy does not promise an unverified deletion deadline for Google service data or website logs.

Messages sent to vibe.captain@yandex.ru, including the sender's email address and information voluntarily included in the message, are used to answer privacy questions. Email is handled through the Yandex mail service. No fixed retention period for these messages has been established. Contact the developer with questions about an existing message; no automatic deletion period is promised.

Firebase requests use HTTPS, and Google describes Play update data as encrypted in transit. Android separates app-private storage from ordinary access by other apps. These measures reduce risk but do not guarantee absolute security. Do not send passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information in privacy enquiries.

You can manage Android backups in your device settings. For questions about processing, access or deletion, contact Vibe Captain using the contact below. Applicable rights and available deletion procedures depend on the information and jurisdiction involved. The game has no user account or account-deletion workflow. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion, to object to or restrict processing, or to complain to a competent data-protection authority. Contact the developer to discuss your request. No consent-based processing or legal basis is asserted by this policy merely because you use the game.
